Beautiful cinematic editorial architectural photograph-illustration, matching a warm traditional Kyoto restaurant. ## food

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: six Japanese food photographs in one precise sprite atlas for a restaurant website. Transparent background. EXACTLY a uniform 3-column by 2-row grid, with equal cells and generous separation. Each cell contains one circular ceramic plate or bowl centered fully within that cell, photographed directly overhead, identical camera scale and soft natural studio lighting, no props extending out of cells. Top row left: salmon nigiri sushi with delicate garnish; top row middle: crispy shrimp and vegetable tempura; top row right: a rich miso ramen bowl with egg and noodles. Bottom row left: colorful Japanese seasonal vegetable and rice bowl; bottom row middle: grilled salmon teriyaki with rice; bottom row right: three delicate matcha mochi with strawberries. Elegant realistic food photography, food vividly appetizing, pottery in ivory and charcoal, soft contact shadows, every plate isolated, no table, no text, no labels, no logos, no people. Landscape 3:2 overall canvas.
